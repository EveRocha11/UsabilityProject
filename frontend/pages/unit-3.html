UNIT 3 (U3)

Grammar and Structures

Passive Voice — Present

How to form and use the present passive: is / are + past participle. "The Eiffel Tower is visited by millions of tourists."

Passive Voice — Past

How to form and use the past passive: was / were + past participle. "The pyramids were built over 4,500 years ago."

Present Perfect with for & since

Expressing how long something has been true: for with a duration ("for decades") and since with a point in time ("since records began").

Cultural & Global Vocabulary

Language for talking about film genres, historical events, art, and entertainment in an international context.
