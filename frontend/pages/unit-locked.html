Grammar and Structures

Gerunds & Infinitives

When to use -ing (enjoy working, avoid making) vs. the infinitive (plan to do, want to be) after common verbs.

Second Conditional

Hypothetical present or future situations: If + past simple, would + base verb. “If I had more time, I would learn coding.”

Third Conditional

Imagined outcomes for past situations: If + past perfect, would have + past participle. “If she had studied, she would have passed.”

Professional Vocabulary

Language for describing skills, job roles, and workplace situations in formal and semi-formal contexts.

Lessons

Identify when to use gerunds or infinitives in professional and everyday contexts.
Practice the Second Conditional to talk about hypothetical situations and wishes.
Master the Third Conditional to reflect on past decisions and imagined outcomes.
